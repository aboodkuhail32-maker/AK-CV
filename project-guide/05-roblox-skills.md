**Tool:** Roblox Studio script editor. Luau is Roblox's faster, typed version of Lua.

What you offer:
- Gameplay & AI systems (NPCs, enemies, pathfinding)
- Inventory & crafting systems
- Combat mechanics
- Roleplay systems
- Optimization & bug fixing
- Custom mechanics & features

Common Roblox pieces these use: `Script` (server), `LocalScript` (client),
`ModuleScript` (shared code), `RemoteEvent` / `RemoteFunction` (client ↔ server
communication), `DataStoreService` (saving player data), `PathfindingService`
(AI movement), `TweenService` (smooth animations).
